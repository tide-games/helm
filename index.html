European single-zero: the spin is blockSeed mod 37, and every one of the felt's 155 legal bets returns exactly 36/37 in expectation — 2.70%, identical across the whole table, enumerated in tests over the maths in helm.js. Tap a pocket on the wheel to bet it and its two neighbours either side. Tap a number for a straight, its edge for a split, a crossing for a corner, the rail on the left for streets (between rows for six lines). Check any Tide spin yourself: the pocket is sha256(blockHash + "|" + mark) read as one big number, mod 37 — tap verify in the log to see every step, or run verifySpin from helm.js.
